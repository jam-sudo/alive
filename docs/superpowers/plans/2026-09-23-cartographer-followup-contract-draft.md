## Minimal question and recommended scope

For one frozen predictor, do prespecified trust scores identify lower measured
prediction-error regions on a common eligible evaluation frame, and what are their
errors, uncertainty and limitations? Predictor optimization, drug expansion, active
acquisition and a new deep architecture are not prerequisites.

Prefer a compatible K562 CRISPRi setting to avoid adding context/modality transfer
claims solely to obtain a benchmark. This is a **source-search preference**, not a
selected asset or assertion that a suitable independent cohort exists. Do not silently
substitute K562 genome-wide for essential, Norman CRISPRa for Replogle CRISPRi, or
activate CT-RPE1. Source compatibility and prior exposure must be established first.

All trust methods operate on the same units, frozen predictions and measured-error
definition: a paired computational comparison, not a randomized intervention trial.
The claim unit must match the actual source/split. Cells and computational seeds are
not independent biological replicates. Target-level and batch/biological dependence
must be documented before choosing any resampling method.

## Required reporting outputs before any run

Use the existing pipeline where compatible; first verify persistence with synthetic
data. Do not run another real comparison whose required outputs would be discarded.

- Frozen input/code/environment identities, exact executed command and terminal status.
- Claim-unit IDs/roles, counts and exclusions, biological/technical hierarchy and
  per-seed handling; paired predictions/errors retained under the approved data policy.
- Raw-scale predictor-error distribution, control/self-distance diagnostics and
  explicit mean-shift/collapse limitations; no distributional-learning claim by default.
- All-method curves, AURC/AUGRC, fixed-coverage risks with actual counts and tie rules;
  all family bounds and separately labeled descriptive intervals.
- Per-seed outputs/variability where prescribed, never counted as independent N;
  registered adequacy evidence, failure/missingness records and uncertainty assumptions.
- Immutable terminal evidence and a pure-reader report. Report generation must not
  fit, select, reopen evaluation outcomes or recompute the scientific verdict.
